😂 How Truth or Bluff Works

Innocents get the Real Prompt while 1 player gets a sneaky Twist Prompt (without knowing they're the Bluffer!). Everyone types a secret answer in 45 seconds, then all answers & the Real Prompt reveal together—bluff in chat/voice and vote out the Bluffer!

💣 Word Chain Category ⏱️ 12s Bomb Per Turn

Type a word starting with the last letter of the previous word before your 12-second bomb hits 0s! No duplicate words allowed. Last player surviving wins!

🎨 How Draw To Find Works

Innocents get the Secret Word while 1 player is The Blender (NO word). On your turn, you have 45 seconds: tap ✏️ Now Draw in the center, draw 1 single stroke, and after 2 full cycles vote out The Blender!
